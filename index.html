Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy special foods, shakes, or supplements?
No. Your plan is built around foods you buy at your regular grocery store, so the habits you learn keep working after you reach your goal.
How often will I need to come in?
We recommend weekly visits for the best results. Each visit is quick and includes a weight and blood pressure check plus a review of your food diary.
Are appetite suppressants required?
No. They are only prescribed when a physician decides they are appropriate for you. If prescribed, a one-week supply is $8 to $16.
What happens at my first appointment?
We take your weight, blood pressure, and measurements, talk about how you eat now, and go over a personalized eating plan with you. The initial consultation is $55.
Can this help with conditions like diabetes or high blood pressure?
Many patients see improvements in blood sugar, blood pressure, and cholesterol as they lose weight. Any changes to your medications should be made by your own physician.
Do you accept insurance?
Please call either office to ask about payment options before your first visit.
